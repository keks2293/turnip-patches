## The fast clear needs the traffic sampler, because a fast clear is a bus event (chain34)

None of the counters above can see the fast clear: it replaces a pixel write with a metadata
write, so its price is on the bus and in the RB's write counters, not in a cycle counter.
`tools/fdperf/fdperf.cfg-traffic` programs those (`RB_Z_WRITE`, the UBWC beats, both VBIF
channels).  Same window and clock rules as the table above; beats are converted at 16 B per
`docs/fdperf-a7xx.md`, the same conversion `scripts/fdperf-traffic-table.py` uses.
